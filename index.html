I'm Anish Kumar Gupta — Social Media Marketer, Content Creator, and Digital Content Strategist. I help brands build a strong online presence through creative content, engaging storytelling, and result-driven social media strategies.

From content creation and scriptwriting to content planning, post design, video editing, and publishing across platforms like Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and LinkedIn, I manage every step of the content journey. I combine creativity with strategy to help businesses increase brand awareness, improve audience engagement, grow their social media presence, and build a recognizable digital identity.
